Process map

Generic version for planning, document tracking, and closing prep.

Before contract

Reservation / prep

  • Confirm the selected homesite and elevation.
  • Review disclosures, HOA documents, and warranty material.
  • Confirm any cancellation deadline that applies.
  • Verify all price assumptions before signing.
Use this step to avoid committing before the full file set is reviewed.
Pre-contract

Loan prep

  • Collect pay stubs, W-2s, asset statements, and liabilities.
  • Keep cash documented and avoid unexplained deposits.
  • Review incentive and rate-lock strategy.
  • Confirm the lender's qualification assumptions.
Keep employment, income, and account history consistent.
Contract signing

Sign and fund

  • Finalize elevation, structural options, and finishes.
  • Sign the agreement and submit required deposits.
  • Track each payment method and date.
  • Save receipts and confirmation emails.
Record every deposit so the cash model stays accurate.
Post-contract

Design studio

  • Review style selections and budget impact.
  • Set cabinets, counters, flooring, tile, and fixture choices.
  • Track any incremental upgrades before final approval.
Update the design budget when selections change.
Build / close

Construction and closing

  • Track construction milestones and lender requests.
  • Compare cash runway against the expected closing month.
  • Confirm wire instructions through a known channel.
  • Review final figures before signing closing documents.
Keep a reserve buffer after closing if possible.
